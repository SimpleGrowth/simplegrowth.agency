Case study

334% more quote leads for an Edinburgh cleaning company

Proshine Specialised Cleaning were paying over £1,000 a month and could not tell what any of it was doing. Here is what we changed.

Sector
Specialised exterior cleaning — domestic and commercial
Area covered
Edinburgh, Fife and the Lothians
What we ran
Website, Google Ads, local SEO, social media, tracking and reporting
Headline result
334% increase in quote leads

Where they started

Proshine were paying Yell more than £1,000 a month to run their website and their Google Ads. What that bought them was a basic Wix site that did very little to turn a visitor into an enquiry, poor rankings for the local searches that actually bring in cleaning work, and a trickle of leads that came nowhere near justifying the spend.

The harder problem was that nobody could say why. Money left the account every month across two channels, and there was no reporting to show which part of it — if any — was working. Without that, there was nothing to fix and nothing to build on.

What we built for them

Five parts, designed to work as one system rather than five separate line items.

  1. 01

    A website built to turn visitors into quotes

    We designed and built proshinespecialisedcleaning.co.uk around the thing that actually matters to a cleaning business — getting someone to ask for a quote. Every service has its own page, from window and gutter cleaning to roof, solar panel and pressure washing, so each one can rank and convert on its own terms.

  2. 02

    Local search across every Edinburgh postcode

    Exterior cleaning is won locally. We built out the Google Business Profile and structured the site's SEO around the areas Proshine actually covers — all seventeen Edinburgh postcode districts, plus Fife and the Lothians — so they show up for the searches that happen street by street.

  3. 03

    Google Ads for the jobs worth bidding on

    Paid search fills the gap while organic rankings build, and keeps the diary full in quieter months. Campaigns are structured by service so budget follows the work Proshine most wants — not whichever keyword happens to be cheapest.

  4. 04

    Social media that shows the work

    We run Instagram and Facebook for Proshine. Exterior cleaning is an unusually visual trade — the before and after does the selling — so the content leans on the transformation rather than on saying how good the service is.

  5. 05

    Closed-loop tracking, so the ads learn

    This is the part most cleaning companies never get. Website forms feed straight into HubSpot, and when a lead actually converts, HubSpot sends that signal back to Google Ads. The campaigns optimise towards enquiries that turn into real jobs, not just anyone who filled in a form. Looker Studio reports pull it together, so Proshine can see what every pound is doing.
