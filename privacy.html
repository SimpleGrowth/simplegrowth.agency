Privacy notice

What we collect, why we collect it, and what you can ask us to do about it.

Last updated 21 August 2026

Who we are

SimpleGrowth is a two-person marketing agency based in Edinburgh, Scotland, operated by Simplegrowth UK Ltd, registered in Scotland with company number 899833. We are the data controller for the personal data described in this notice, which means we decide what is collected and why.

For anything to do with your data, email us at getsimplegrowth@gmail.com, or write to us at our registered office: 109/14 Swanston Road, Edinburgh, EH10 7DS, United Kingdom.

What we collect

When you fill in a form

Our contact form asks for your first name, last name, email address, business name and your message. Our request a call form asks for the same, plus your phone number.

We use this only to reply to you and to talk about working together. We do not add you to a marketing list from a form submission, and we do not sell or share your details with anyone for their own marketing.

When you email us

If you email us directly we hold that correspondence, along with your email address and anything you choose to tell us in it.

When you browse the site

If you accept analytics cookies, we collect statistics about how the site is used — which pages are visited, roughly where in the world visitors are, and what kind of device they are on.

This data is pseudonymous rather than anonymous. It is tied to a random identifier stored in your browser, not to your name, so we cannot tell who you are from it and we do not try to. It still counts as personal data under UK data protection law, which is exactly why we ask your permission before collecting any of it.

Our host also keeps standard server logs, which include IP addresses, as part of delivering the site securely. The site loads its typefaces from Google Fonts, which means your IP address is visible to Google when a page loads.

Cookies

The site sets no cookies at all until you accept them. There are no advertising or tracking cookies on this site.

If you accept, Google Analytics sets cookies beginning _ga, which distinguish one visitor from another and expire after up to two years. Your accept or decline choice is remembered in your browser's local storage so we do not ask again on every page.

You can change your mind whenever you like using the Cookie settings link at the bottom of any page. Declining after having accepted also deletes the analytics cookies already set.

Our legal bases

Under UK GDPR we need a lawful basis for using your data. Ours are:

  • Consent — for analytics cookies. You give it by accepting the banner, and you can withdraw it at any time.
  • Taking steps at your request before entering a contract — for replying to your enquiry.
  • Legitimate interests — for keeping our own business records, and for the server logs that keep the site online and secure.

Who else handles your data

We keep the list of third parties deliberately short. Each one only processes data on our instructions:

  • HubSpot — stores the enquiries sent through our forms. Our HubSpot account is hosted in the European Union.
  • Google Analytics — provides the usage statistics, and only if you accept cookies.
  • Google — provides our email, and serves the typefaces the site uses.
  • GitHub — hosts the website itself.

Data sent outside the UK

Some of those providers are based in the United States, so some data is transferred outside the UK. Where that happens it is covered by the safeguards UK data protection law requires, such as the UK extension to the EU–US Data Privacy Framework or standard contractual clauses. Enquiries submitted through our forms are held in HubSpot's EU-hosted infrastructure.

If you would like a copy of the safeguards covering a particular transfer, email us and we will send it to you.

How long we keep it

  • Enquiries and correspondence — up to 24 months after we last hear from you, unless you become a client.
  • Client records — for the duration of our work together, and then for up to 6 years, which is how long UK tax and accounting rules require business records to be kept.
  • Analytics data — 14 months, after which Google deletes it automatically.

Automated decisions

We do not make decisions about you by automated means, and we do not profile you. Nothing on this site scores, ranks or sorts people automatically.

Your rights

Under UK GDPR you can ask us to:

  • give you a copy of the personal data we hold about you
  • correct anything that is wrong
  • delete your data, where we have no continuing reason to keep it
  • restrict or object to how we are using it
  • send your data to you or another provider in a portable format
  • withdraw consent you previously gave, at any time

Email getsimplegrowth@gmail.com and we will respond within one month. There is no charge for making a request.

Complaints

If you think we have handled your data badly, please tell us first so we can put it right. You also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner's Office, the UK's data protection regulator, at ico.org.uk or on 0303 123 1113.

Changes to this notice

If we change how we handle personal data we will update this page and change the date at the top of it.
